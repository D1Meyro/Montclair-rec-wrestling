MORE THAN A SPORT

Strong wrestlers.
Even stronger kids.

Montclair Rec Wrestling helps young athletes learn fundamentals, build confidence, and find a supportive team.

A place to learn and grow.

Wrestling gives kids a chance to discover what they can do—one practice, one challenge, and one small victory at a time.

Our program is about putting in the effort and supporting each other. We celebrate progress, encourage sportsmanship, and help young athletes grow in confidence on and off the mat.
