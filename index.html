MORE THAN A SPORT

Strong wrestlers.
Even stronger kids.

Wrestling gives kids a chance to discover what they can do—one practice, one challenge, and one small victory at a time.

Montclair Rec Wrestling is about learning fundamentals, putting in the effort, and supporting each other. We celebrate progress, encourage sportsmanship, and help young athletes grow in confidence on and off the mat.

GOOD TO KNOW

A little less wondering.
A little more wrestling.

Does my child need experience?

No experience is needed for the October 19 clinic. It is specifically for beginners in grades 2–8, rather than experienced wrestlers.

Can girls participate?

Absolutely. The beginner clinic welcomes both girls and boys in grades 2–8.

How do we register for the winter season?

Contact us for current season registration details, fees, practice times, and eligibility. The clinic sign-up is separate from season registration.
